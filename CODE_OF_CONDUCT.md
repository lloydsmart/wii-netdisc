# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

Wii NetDisc welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels.
Everyone should be able to participate without harassment or discrimination,
including on the basis of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or
expression, nationality, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Treat people with respect and give constructive, specific feedback.
- Keep disagreements focused on the work and the evidence.
- Make room for questions, different perspectives and honest mistakes.
- Respect people's privacy and boundaries.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, make personal attacks, give unwanted
  sexual attention, publish private information without permission, or repeatedly
  disrupt participation.
- Do not retaliate against someone for raising a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This policy applies to everyone, including the maintainer, in this repository's
issues, pull requests and other project spaces. It also applies when someone
officially represents Wii NetDisc elsewhere.

## Reporting concerns

Report conduct concerns privately to Lloyd Smart at
[conduct@lloydsmart.com](mailto:conduct@lloydsmart.com). Lloyd is currently the
project's sole maintainer and receives these reports.

Include a brief account of what happened, relevant links or dates, and any
immediate concern or help you need. Share only the personal information needed
to understand the report; do not put sensitive details in a public issue.

Reports will be handled discreetly, with information shared only as needed to
assess and respond to the concern. There is no guaranteed response time.

For behaviour on GitHub that violates its platform policies, including a concern
involving the maintainer, you can use
[GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
GitHub handles platform-policy reports; this project has no separate conduct
committee or independent appeal panel.

Report security vulnerabilities through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

The maintainer may ask someone to stop or correct behaviour, issue a warning,
remove inappropriate content, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation.
The response should reflect the seriousness, impact and repetition of the conduct.
People affected by a decision may use the private reporting address to provide
additional information or request reconsideration.
